Welcome to Wavecraft

Wavecraft is a free, open-source per-app audio mixer for macOS — per-app volume, 10-band EQ, and per-app output routing, the things paid apps like SoundSource charge for.

This installer will set up:

  • Wavecraft, the menu bar app you'll actually use
  • A virtual audio device that sits between your apps and your speakers, which is how per-app control is possible at all on macOS
  • A small background helper the app uses to talk to that device

You'll need to enter your admin password, and Wavecraft will restart your Mac's audio for a moment near the end of installation — any audio playing at that point may briefly cut out.

Once it's done, Wavecraft opens on its own and walks you through the couple of permissions it needs, and exactly what each one is for.
